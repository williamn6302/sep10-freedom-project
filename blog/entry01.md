# Entry 1: Deciding on my topic (Technology in music)
##### 10/24/26

## Choosing a Topic
I have always had an appreciation towards music. When we started this project my mind instantly jumped to how technology could be used in music and to make music. As I researched more about this topic I came across certain systems like the [ Head Rush digital pedal board ](https://www.headrushfx.com/products/pedalboard/index.html). This device emulates the sound of many different pedals effects and can emulate amplifiers. This innovation allows for a more efficient way of detailing one’s music and tone. Some people also don’t realize how much of technology already goes into music. Different softwares like [BandLAB](https://www.bandlab.com/?lang=en) allow people to make their own music online as well as have digital instruments to record sounds. Softwares like these have existed for a long time now.

## Engineering and Design Process 
I am currently in the first stage of EDP. The objective of EDP is to identify a problem. The problem I wish to identify is people’s lack of knowledge of how technology impacts music and the music making process. The next step is to research the problem and I am planning to  not only use articles on websites, but to also talk to my classmates and those around me to discuss the topic and gain their insight on the situation.
