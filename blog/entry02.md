##### 11/22/26
## Content
Throughout the research I have found several examples of software and hardware. Softwares like [BandLab](bandlab.com/?lang=en), [Virtual Piano](https://virtualpiano.net/), and [Sound Trap](https://www.soundtrap.com/musicmakers) are great for people trying to create their own sounds without a whole bunch of equipment. Hardware like [Guitar/Bass pickups](https://www.seymourduncan.com/products/pickups/bass/jazz-bass), [Fx pedals](https://www.guitarcenter.com/Effects-Pedals.gc) and [Amplifiers](https://www.fender.com/products/bassman-610-neo-enclosure?variant=45940649165022) allow musicians to customize how they are heard and how they can show their creativity. To find this I searched music software and music hardware. These searches led me to websites like BandLab as well as websites to purchase things like pedals. When organizing the research I decided to make 2 categories; technology used by musicians and technology used to fully record a song. The reason I did this is because things like amplifiers, pedals, pickups and the virtual piano are all instrument related. Bandlab and Soundtrap are used to produce full on songs.
