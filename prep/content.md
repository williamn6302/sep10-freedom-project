##### Hardware
* Bass/guitar Pickups
  * These are little things with magnets called poll pieces. These magnets "pick up" the vibrations of the __metallic__ strings. After they convert it into an electrical current sent through a coil(s) that is converted into a sound once it reaches a speaker of some sort.
  * Different pickups offer different sounds and tone depending on placement, axis of pole pieces, or size of pole pieces and the design of the coil of inside of it.
  * [Seymour Duncan Pickups](https://www.seymourduncan.com/products/pickups/bass/jazz-bass)
*   Amplifier
  *  A "amp" takes the signal of the instrument sent to it via electrical signals and can increase it's volume or just convert it into sound without enhancing anything.
  *  Some amps don't have a speaker and act as a accessory to the instrument
  *  If it's a combo amp it has a speaker attached to it using a wire voice coil suspended in a magnetic field. When the coil receives the electrical signal, it causes the cone to move back and forth, creating a pressure wave, better known as a sound wave.
  *  [Fender Amp](https://www.fender.com/products/bassman-610-neo-enclosure?variant=45940649165022)
* Microphones
  * Microphones are used to make a persons voice audible through a speaker or a mixing system.
  * They convert a sound picked up into an electrical current similarly to the pickups for the bass and guitar.
  * [Microphone](https://www.bestbuy.com/product/blue-microphones-blue-yeti-professional-multi-pattern-usb-condenser-microphone-blackout/J39723G2GZ/sku/4758301?utm_source=feed&extStoreId=482)
* Pedals and other Fx
  * A Fx can change the sound of a instrument
  * These are used typically with pianos basses and guitars and can be hardware or software
  * Typical pedals and Fx are sustain which keep a sound playing even when the musician stops, and delay which delays the timing from when the instrument is played to when the sound comes out the output
  * Fx pedals work by modulating the signal given by your instrument
  * There are volume pedals that increase or decrease volume so you can keep playing while adjusting volume.
  * [Fx pedals on Guitar Center](https://www.guitarcenter.com/Effects-Pedals.gc)
